## A finding worth the product owner's attention: the installer and the .dmg

**The app keeps `data/` and `exports/` beside its own executable**
(`src-tauri/src/paths.rs`) — that is the spec's design, and it is what makes the
synced folder the teacher's agenda. It follows that **an installed copy keeps her
data outside the synced folder**: the NSIS installer puts the app in
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Teacher Planner`, and the `.dmg` invites dragging it into
`/Applications`. The pre-M10 guide said SmartScreen would appear "only on the
installer, once" — which implied she would install it.

So: the guide now tells her to run **`teacher-planner.exe` and `Teacher
Planner.app` from the synced folder** and not to use the installer or the `.dmg`.
The brief asked for both in the handover folder, so they are there, in a folder
of their own called `installers/`. **Whether to hand them over at all is the
product owner's call** (Open question 1). One consequence for signing is now
written into `docs/CODE_SIGNING.md`: the exe she double-clicks must itself be
signed, and SmartScreen meets that exe (if it carries Mark of the Web), not an
installer run once.

## The VM, and why the Windows half did not run

At the start of M10, `ssh -p 2222 kyria@127.0.0.1` timed out during the banner
exchange, repeatedly. The VM was `running`, its desktop up, its clock matching
the Mac's (12:46 on both). With the product owner's go-ahead for a graceful
reboot, an ACPI power-button press was sent and **ignored** by Windows for five
minutes; the agent then opened *Run* through VirtualBox's keyboard input and
entered `shutdown /r /t 0` (13:42). The product owner, at the console, then
found in an Administrator PowerShell that **`Get-Service sshd` reports no such
service** — only `ssh-agent` is registered — which is the half-removed state
`docs/WINDOWS_VM.md` §3b describes, and that the guest's folders were in no
state to reinstall from. The product owner chose to proceed Mac only.

**Worth knowing for the next milestone:** `clean-baseline` (M8, toolchain and
sshd working, before the app ran) is still the only snapshot, and reverting to
it is the documented per-milestone routine. It would discard everything done in
the guest since M8; the M9 helpers survive on the Mac in M9's scratchpad.
